About Me

Where logic meets the browser

I’m currently a 2nd year Mathematics student at Surya Sen Mahavidyalaya. Somewhere between proofs and problem sets, I got interested in how websites are actually built — and I started teaching myself by making things.

Web development is still a field I’m exploring, not one I’ve mastered, and I’m okay saying that. What I bring from Mathematics is a habit of breaking problems into smaller steps, checking my work, and not being afraid of getting something wrong before I get it right — the same approach that helps when a project doesn’t run the first time.

My goal is simple: keep building real, practical projects, keep learning the tools of the trade, and steadily grow from a student who codes into a developer who studied Mathematics.
